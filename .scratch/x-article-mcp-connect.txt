⚡ Connect Your AI Agent to GammaRips in 5 Minutes

GammaRips is an overnight options-flow engine. Every night it scans the US options market and cuts thousands of tickers down to a small pool with unusual bullish flow. Then it tracks what every one of those contracts actually did, from the morning it surfaced to expiration. Losers included.

Your agent can query all of it over MCP (Model Context Protocol, the standard way to wire live data into an AI agent).

We sell data, not picks. Your agent reads the pool, the outcome history, and the methodology, then reasons to its own conclusions.


🚀 What Your Agent Gets

• 29 tools covering the curated pool, per-contract detail, outcome history, and market context.
• The honest record: about 60% of pool contracts touch +40% at some point. The median still expires worthless. Your agent can verify that itself, query by query.
• Research tools: replay any past contract and test an exit rule against the actual tape before trusting it.


🛠 Step 1: Connect Free. No Account Needed.

Claude Code (one line in your terminal):

claude mcp add --transport http gammarips https://gammarips-mcp-406581297632.us-central1.run.app/mcp

Any MCP client (JSON config):

{
  "mcpServers": {
    "gammarips": {
      "url": "https://gammarips-mcp-406581297632.us-central1.run.app/mcp"
    }
  }
}

Also on the official MCP Registry (io.github.DevDizzle/gammarips) and Smithery. The free tier gives your agent a taste of the pool.

✅ Step 3: Test It

Ask your agent:

"Pull today's GammaRips pool and summarize the flow."


🧰 Tools Worth Knowing (of the 29)

• get_enriched_signals - today's curated pool with scores and flow stats
• get_opportunity_surface - how far each past contract ran before it died
• query_outcomes / get_outcome_summary - the full outcome history, wins and losses together
• replay_contract / estimate_exit_rule - test an exit idea against real history
• get_regime_context / get_earnings_window - the safety checks we gate on


🎯 Prompts to Try

• "Which names in today's pool have the strongest flow?"
• "Pull the outcome history for contracts like the top name. What usually happens?"
• "Simulate a +40% take-profit rule against the pool's history. What does it miss?"


🛠 Troubleshooting

• 401 Unauthorized: the header is Authorization: Bearer gr_live_...
• Regenerated your key? The old one dies instantly. Update your config.
• Canceled? Keys deactivate within about 2 minutes.
