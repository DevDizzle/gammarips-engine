P3. GAP-018 IS STILL UNSHIPPED (and today it got lucky)
====================================================================

Root cause from 08-07 stands: signal-notifier/main.py _fetch_live_oi
(~L1361) reads day.volume from the Polygon option snapshot but never
reads day.last_updated, so on a contract with no prints today Polygon
serves the PRIOR session's day bar and yesterday's volume is stamped
"confirmed" as this morning's prints.

TODAY IT DID NOT MISFIRE. The 2026-08-10 card (PLTR, 09:53 ET) claimed
"Liquidity: 1009 prints by ~09:52 (confirmed)" and the claim held up:
get_liquidity showed day_volume accumulating on a day.last_updated of
today, and the Robinhood book independently showed 4,668 prints by 12:33
against OI 7,354.

We are reporting the clean day so the record is symmetric. It is not
evidence the bug is gone -- PLTR was a heavily-traded name on a strong
tape, which is precisely the case the bug cannot manifest in. The failure
needs a zero-print name to show up, and one will come.

Still asking for the bar-date validation (a timestamp check, not price
leakage). With PRINT_FLOOR_MIN=1 already in place, validating the date
drops zero-print names before the tournament ever ranks them.
