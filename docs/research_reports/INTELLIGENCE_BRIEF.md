## 2026-08-19 update — Selection re-scored on the TRADEABLE subset (N=3,776 / 87 days): the ghosts were FLATTERING the pool. Honest composite is -9.6%, not -4.7%. Pre-committed contract_score re-test FAILS

**Owner call: if ~63% of the pool is untradeable by 10:00 ET, re-score selection with the ghosts stripped out. The result inverts the hypothesis.** Stripping ghosts makes the measured composite WORSE, monotonically: 0 prints by 10:00 -2.23%, 3-5 -5.46%, 11-20 -9.81%, 21+ -9.17%. Whole pool -4.67%, **TRADEABLE (11+ prints) -9.59% [-13.4, -5.7]**.

**The mechanism is measurement, and it is verified.** 33.7% of 0-print rows carry the EXACT no-move return of **-1.9608%** (= 1/1.02 - 1: the exit filling at the entry bar's own close because nothing printed in between). 16.8% of the whole pool is that one value. Ghost rows are 70.6% `illiquid_exit` and 86.0% TIMEOUT, and the gradient SURVIVES deleting every `illiquid_exit` row, because production's flag catches only 37% of ghosts. The ghost tier looks good purely through **non-participation**: its bracket fires on 8.8% of rows versus 35.8% on tradeable, so it exits at a stale mark worth -1.5%. That is the absence of a trade, not performance. **Consequence: every whole-pool performance number this program has published is optimistic by construction.**

**Stripping ghosts did NOT reveal a hidden selection edge.** 14 leakage-safe features x pooled and day-demeaned AUC x 2 subsets = 56 looks; only 3 CIs excluded 0.50, and 56 looks at a 90% CI produce ~5.6 spurious exclusions by chance. Three hits is BELOW chance. No ranking power.

**Pre-committed re-test FAILED and closes a hypothesis.** The 2026-08-05 flagged lead (cap-50-era `contract_score` 0.552 pooled / 0.564 day-demeaned) was to be re-tested on >=15 fresh closed-label days after 07-27. The days now exist (N=737, 15 days) and it reads **0.481 [0.457, 0.505] pooled, 0.484 demeaned** — below 0.50. Clean out-of-sample rejection. Close it; do not re-slice the cap-50 era for it again.

**The tournament pick tilts toward tradeable but not nearly enough.** Picks land on TRADEABLE 32.8% of the time versus a 15.8% pool base (2.1x lift, the live OI + print floors working), yet **48.2% of picks still land on 0-2-print contracts**. Pick beats the same-day pool by +0.84pp overall (n=58) and +4.41pp on the tradeable subset, but that second number is n=19 with a CI crossing zero. Directional only.

**The one constructive finding: the surface is real and the FIXED bracket is what destroys it.** Same-day excursion on tradeable contracts (the correct window; the 3-day `opp_*` surface inverts this comparison exactly as memory `same-day-vs-3day-window-mismatch` warns): median MFE **+10.6%** against a +40% target reached 12.7% of the time, median MAE **-23.2%** with -30% visited **37.6%** of the time. Unconditional same-day touches: +20% is hit 32%, +40% 13%; -30% 38%, -50% 13%. Read as SHAPE, not EV — touch rates ignore path order and [[bracket-optimization-dead]] is settled (0/840 variants profitable). What is new is that the 840-variant sweep ran on `signals_labeled_v1`, a whole-pool cohort now shown to be ~58% fabricated-flat, and no bracket work had run on a tradeability-labelled subset when this was written. **Answered the same day: the 0/432 sweep (entry below) closes it.** Full tables: `FINDINGS_LEDGER.md` §2026-08-19 (tradeable subset).

## 2026-08-19 update — PM entry / "catch the morning pop" REFUTED (N=1,074 legs / 64 days): no morning pop exists on tradeable contracts, and the overnight hold deletes the stop

**Owner question: enter in the afternoon and catch the next morning pop, instead of entering in the morning? Answer: no, on both halves of the premise.** Replayed the live V7.1 bracket (+40% / -30%, 2% slippage each way) on `option_minute_paths` (4,292 legs, 87 scan dates, 04-10 -> 08-17) under A = LIVE 10:00 -> 15:45 same day, B = PM 15:45 -> next-day exit at six times, and C = CONTROL 10:00 entry held to day-2 10:00. Cadence is one trade per day in every arm, so this is a pure EV plus tail comparison and the V7 velocity lever does not decide it.

**Half one: the pop is a penny-option artifact.** Raw day-2 09:30 -> 10:00 reads mean +8.19% across all legs, but the median is exactly **+0.00%** and two sub-$0.60 contracts (MBLY +2,100%, CHWY +1,400%) carry the mean. On liquid contracts (>=120 traded min/day, premium >= $1, N=103) the overnight gap is **-4.43%** and the morning half-hour is **-3.60%**. Both legs of the "afternoon in, morning out" trade are negative exactly where you can transact.

**Half two: the overnight hold deletes the stop, and that is the decisive number.** On the entry-knowable liquid tier (day-1 liquidity only, N=168/45d) the live arm's p05 is **-31.4%** because the -30% stop IS the floor. Every overnight arm prints p05 near **-53%**: options do not trade overnight, so the stop cannot execute in the gap. The tail deepens about 22 percentage points while the mean falls from -4.42% to -9.86%.

**Control arm C is the cleanest result: the damage is the HOLD, not the entry time.** C changes only the hold and is significantly worse in both liquid tiers (-5.55% [-10.1, -1.6] and -4.87% [-8.7, -1.4], CIs exclude zero). That independently re-confirms [[v7-gigo-same-day-exit]] on a question that study never asked.

**Sign flips with liquidity, which is the recurring tell.** The paired PM-minus-LIVE difference is +2.03% [-2.1, +6.0] on all fillable legs and **-5.44% [-15.0, +4.2]** on the entry-knowable liquid tier. The apparent gain lives entirely in contracts nobody can trade. This is the same thin-tape mirage the 2026-06-22 entry-timing study hit, and this session reproduced the failure mode in code before catching it: version 1 of the replay carried the last close forward at a missing timeout print, which invented near-flat exits for illiquid legs and read as PM +1.55%. Requiring a real print within 15 minutes at both ends of every arm reversed the conclusion. **Rule for any future replay on this substrate: 73% of its bars are single-print and the median day-2 leg has 11 traded minutes out of 390, so a carried-forward price is a fabricated fill.**

**Decision: keep V7.1 unchanged. No PM entry, no overnight hold. That question is CLOSED.** Hold the two entry facts apart: PM entry and the overnight hold are REFUTED here, and the separate later-SAME-DAY entry question is an OPEN owner call (see the entry-hour update below). Caveats: single regime, liquid N=103-168, no NBBO so no arm pays a spread. Entry/exit-timing finding only, it does not bear on selection. Full tables: `FINDINGS_LEDGER.md` §2026-08-19. Script: `backtesting_and_research/2026-08-19_pm_entry_overnight_study.py`.

## 2026-08-19 update — Execution-risk calibration: the stop is the failure point, not the spread

**Owner question: what guidelines minimize execution risk for the autonomous traders? Answer: manage exit certainty, not spread.** We hold NO spread data (`bid`/`ask`/`mid`/`spread_pct` NULL in all 64,550 `pool_liquidity_snapshot` reads), so every number is a print-based proxy. The median pool leg trades 9 minutes out of 390. Stop fills slip one-sided: median **-3.1%** past the -30% level across N=547 breach events, median **-10.3%** on sub-10-print names, worst -44.0%; 33.6% of stops fill more than 5% past the level. **Prints by 10:00 ET is the dominant, near-deterministic observable** (0 early prints gives 0.0% chance of 60+ prints later; 21+ gives 93.0%), and premium does NOT predict liquidity (contracts over $5 print least). Modelling round-trip spread is second-order: the problem is "you cannot transact when you need to", not "you pay a wide spread twice". Guidelines: `docs/EXECUTION-RISK-GUIDELINES.md` ([[execution-risk-is-exit-certainty]]). Full tables: `FINDINGS_LEDGER.md` §2026-08-19 (execution).

## 2026-08-19 update — Bracket sweep re-run on the tradeable subset: 0/432 profitable. [[bracket-optimization-dead]] replicates on the honest substrate

**The ghost-contamination objection to the 0/840 verdict is CLOSED.** 432 same-day variants (target x stop x exit time, no-target and no-stop included, real prints required at both anchors) ran on TRADEABLE (591 legs / 50 days) and SEMI (985 legs): **0 positive full-period, 0 in-sample, 0 out-of-sample, on both tiers.** The grid is not noise (cross-half rank correlation of variant means 0.575 / 0.581), there is simply nothing above zero to find. The best variants (~-6.0%) all sit ON the tightest-stop edge of the grid (-15% stop, midday exit), the signature of an optimum outside the grid: "do not hold the position at all." **Do not re-cut the live bracket to -15%**: a tighter stop fires more often and pays the measured stop slippage more often, so the sweep's -6.0% is optimistic. Doctrine now holds on every substrate measured; the open question that actually decides pick quality is the pool-versus-benchmark test. Full tables: `FINDINGS_LEDGER.md` §2026-08-19 (bracket sweep, tradeable).

## 2026-08-19 update — Entry hour: 10:00 is the single worst hour of the day. Later same-day entry is an OPEN owner call (flagged once)

**On the tradeable subset (N=548) the 10:00 to 11:00 window reads -5.32% mean, while the other six windows sum to about -2.5%. The entire day's bleed is the first hour.** A 12:00 entry keeps most of the opportunity (median MFE +8.8% vs +12.8% at 10:00) and skips the -5.32% hour. This corroborates the 2026-06-22 study on the tradeable cell it lacked power for (N=55 then, N=548 now): earlier-than-10:00 stays closed as a thin-tape mirage, the LATER-entry question is new and open. **No code change made; the owner call stays open** ([[first-hour-bleed]]).
