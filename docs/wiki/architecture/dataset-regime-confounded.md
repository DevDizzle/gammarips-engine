(Feb 28), Strait of Hormuz closure (Mar 4), Brent → $120, VIX peak 35.3, IV–RV spread ~9pts
(3× normal), March term-structure backwardation, V-shaped reversal Mar 31. Every conclusion
of the form "filter X works" or "bracket Y fails" from this window is conflated with "this
regime broke long-options strategies in general."

([[volatility-idiosyncratic-trap]]) and the record variance-risk premium of the window. Hard
rule: do NOT draw signal-quality conclusions from this 6-week window alone, and do NOT deploy
any strategy backtested only on it. Edge is earned FORWARD, confirmed at N≥15 live. This is
why the ongoing option-PnL substrate exists and why [[labeled-v1-screen-not-validator]].
