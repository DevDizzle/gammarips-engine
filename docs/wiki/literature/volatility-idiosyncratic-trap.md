# Volatility-idiosyncratic trap — UOA scanners select the most overpriced volatility

Overnight-gap and UOA scanners systematically select stocks with extreme idiosyncratic
volatility; options on those names are systematically OVERpriced by market makers as
compensation for hedging difficulty (Cao & Han 2013: long delta-hedged options on
high-idiosyncratic-vol names earn ~−1.4%/month). We are buying the most overpriced volatility
in our universe at the moment the variance risk premium is highest — the directional read can
be real while the option premium consumes the edge before the move plays out.

Confirmed on our own data: option-side bleed −3.26% vs stock 1× −0.33% and SPY −0.21% — the
INSTRUMENT alone accounts for ~89% of the bleed (−2.93pp/trade). This is the mechanistic
reason bracket optimization is a dead end ([[bracket-optimization-dead]]), the pool is
merely fairly-priced ([[pool-delta-calibrated]]), and all ROI must come from selection +
exit craft, not from holding the option ([[fixed-exit-composites-negative]]).
