# 3-day harvest curve — the pop is real but LATE and not rule-able

Over a 3-day harvest window on the enriched pool: **P(touch +20% in 3d) ≈ 51%**, but the
pops land day 2–3, NOT day 1; fixed profit targets are EV-negative pool-wide; and **−30%
is touched on half of paths**. So a mechanical fixed-target harvest rule does not extract
the excursion — consistent with the giveback ([[path-calibrated-giveback]]) and negative
fixed-exit composites ([[fixed-exit-composites-negative]]).

The one positive-EV lead was the subset of **tournament picks** (N=24) — but that N is far
too small to be a finding; hold it as a lead, not a claim. The through-expiry MFE ceiling is
NOT computable from stored data (`opp_window_days`=3); it needs the multi-day / to-expiration
follow collector.
