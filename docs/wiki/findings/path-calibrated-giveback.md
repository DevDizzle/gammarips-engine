# The pool is PATH-calibrated too — the GIVEBACK is the real finding

Realized intrinsic-bound excursion peaks sit at the **50.95th percentile** of their own
IV-implied distributions (CI straddles 0.5; KS p=0.50), and peak TIMING matches the null
(median day 7 of a 10-day life; peaks arrive LATE). So there is **no IV-beating anomaly to
claim** — the pool is calibrated on the path as well as at expiry ([[pool-delta-calibrated]]).

The durable, product-relevant finding is the **GIVEBACK**: P(peak ≥ +100%) = 36%, but
conditional on peak ≥ +50% the median contract retains only **31% of its peak** at expiry,
and 37.8% round-trip to a loss; **48.5% of ever-profitable contracts die at a loss**. The
verified product story is a high-excursion, fairly-priced surface plus exit craft — which is
exactly why the exit is a same-day get-in-get-out ([[v7-gigo-same-day-exit]]) and why the
whole-pool fixed-exit composite is negative ([[fixed-exit-composites-negative]]).
