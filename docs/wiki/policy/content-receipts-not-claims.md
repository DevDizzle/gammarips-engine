# Public content posts RECEIPTS, not picks or claims

Public content is framed as **receipts, not claims**. The X SIGNAL post is anchor-only
(Path B): it names ticker + direction + score and WITHHOLDS the contract/strike/expiration,
so a closing-trade callback has a public timestamped post to quote-tweet as a receipt rather
than a performance claim. Reddit posts are short (~230 char) first-person distraction-frame —
no methodology walk-through, no literature citations (long cited posts get karma-killed;
receipts compound).

The full-bracket curated pick stays off public surfaces entirely ([[public-tracks-pool-not-pick]])
— which under the current posture means the daily pick is private (scalping optics) and the
public receipts are pool-level ([[x-poster-revamp-agentic]]). Disclaimer treatment: "Paper
trade. Not advice." appears only on performance posts (win/loss/callback/scorecard).
